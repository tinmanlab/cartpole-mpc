Model and experiment boundary

Source-derived
Upkie pinned geometry: 0.25 m leg links, 0.05 m wheel radius, hip/knee limit 16 N·m, wheel limit 1.7 N·m. Single-side sagittal derivative with primitive geometry and checked primitive inertias.
Declared assumptions
Torso/rod/tire dimensions and masses are selected source values; motor assemblies are omitted. Primitive decomposition, contact, damping, sensors, noise, home pose and control weights are declared assumptions. The sketch supplies none of these numbers. See model notes.

State and sensing

Plant state: x, z, pitch, hip, knee, wheel angle and their six velocities (12 values). The local KF receives noisy external x/z localization, torso orientation, and hip/knee measurements; it does not receive true velocities. This is a visible teaching localization channel, not IMU-plus-encoders-only feasibility. Wheel encoder angle cannot recover absolute x during slip.

Contact and limits

Contact and slip will be reported from MuJoCo after model validation.

Actuator limits: hip ±16, knee ±16, wheel ±1.7 N·m.

Standing modes use one shared seeded localization/KF estimator for LQR and MPC. MPC is a torque-constrained local linear finite-horizon controller (N = 20, 0.2 s); it has no state, contact, or friction constraints and no robustness guarantee.

Planned jump

Reset & jump starts a separate four-second task at its declared initial posture. The nonlinear reference supplies motor feedforward torques; time-varying LQR and a scheduled KF use five noisy position measurements for feedback. All three motor limits remain unchanged. No upward force, hidden support, or mid-flight state reset is used. The verified plan applies to this local model task; arbitrary impacts and hardware performance are unverified.
